    How to play

    Modeled on 10-2: HME rescue pumper, Hale Q-Flo 1250 GPM, 800-gallon tank, FRC pressure governor, Hale MIV electric master intake valve.

    Crack + opens a valve a quarter turn at a time; Gate − closes it a quarter turn, to give one line less pressure than another. Snap throws it wide open — water hammer, or a lost prime at draft. The MIV travels on its own; that's what it's for.

    Three tiers per scenario: Guided shows every step and the target pressure. Recall hides the checklist and makes you figure the PDP from the hose layout. Chaos is Recall plus things going wrong.

    New: Panel guide. Tap Panel guide (top right) during any mission, then tap any control to learn what it does, when to use it, and what goes wrong. Free in Guided, 2 points per lookup in Recall, locked in Chaos.

    Training aid only. Department SOPs and the preconnect chart on the rig are the real numbers.

# Charge the Line — changelog

Pump panel simulator for Engine 10-2. Plain-language history, newest first. Practice, not certification.

## 2.24.0 — October 18, 2026
- One Pump math drill (eight multiple-choice questions: four pump-pressure problems, four tank-time problems) replaces the typing drill and Tank time, so Charge the Line counts 20 activities like every other module.
- Saved runs of Tank time and the old Pump math merge into one Pump math record; the best score is kept. The old Tank time link opens Pump math.

## 2.23.2 — October 11, 2026
- The foam proportioner's and the eductor's percentage chips are finger-sized (the 1% chips were 42 px wide).

## 2.23.1 — October 11, 2026
- Found by the every-activity gate: the governor can reach 400 psi on the First Interstate two-stage pumper, so its 315–345 psi standpipe band is reachable with the buttons (it was capped at 300).

## 2.23.0 — October 11, 2026 — tester release
- Every activity (the lesson, five drills, Pump math and all fourteen scenarios) is played to the end with real taps at 390 px before a release.
- CHANGELOG added; the post-pilot backlog recorded in CLAUDE.md.

## 2.22.0 — October 10, 2026
- A decision card's options and the result row ignore taps for about half a second after they appear, so a finger still moving from the last tap cannot answer by accident.
- A check keeps every button that refuses an action down to physical prerequisites (no tanker to connect, no water to bleed, and so on).

## 2.21.0 to 2.21.2 — October 10, 2026 — one voice
- Start on the briefing, Stop and go back on the panel, Again · Home · Next on every result, ◂ Preconnect · Feedback under every result, About with the version line.
- The EV fire's tanker wait fast-forwards once tankers are requested.
- Links open a scenario, the lesson or Pump math directly (for the hub's Continue).
- "New version available — tap to reload" when a new offline copy takes over.

## 2.19.1 to 2.20.0 — October 10, 2026 — final sweep
- A sticky status strip under the header shows the current step and the crew's last line while a scenario runs.
- The governor's buttons are two rows, all finger-sized; gauge labels 13 px; the text floor everywhere.
- Pump math counts as the 21st activity (a set of five or more typed answers).
- The gauges draw once and only the needle moves; the loop rests at the menu.
- The offline helper loads the page and the shared core network-first, then the saved copy.
- A readiness card and the standard home layout shared with every module.
